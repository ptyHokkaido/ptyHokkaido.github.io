Zephyr has so much quality content in it that I just cant do anything else but recommend it if you want to step up your Active Directory game from beginner to intermediate, it is also a very realistic content in my opinion and does have quite a nice learning curve, very well designed lab.

Took me two months to take it down but Im sure you could do it in just one if you focus enough on it, my favorite thing from it was to work with forests since its a must for real engagements and its something you wont find easily in other labs yet it can be quite tricky to work across if you're first timing, also the way you've to understand your pivots and where you are since there are firewalls in place protecting the machines. I'd point way more stuff I liked but cant say much more since it would be spoilers.

Saying this I previously did the AD module from HTB as well and of course many AD machines, I think anyone else that would like to try this lab would need at least some beginner knowledge to start with or else you will just perma bang your head against a big wall.


tl;dr: 100% recommended but get some AD knowledge before going for it.
