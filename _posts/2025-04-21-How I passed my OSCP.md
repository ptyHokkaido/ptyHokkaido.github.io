That moment has finally arrived, the post about my own OSCP journey. I could type for hours about what preparation I had and whatnot but thats kinda already on my previous post about the certification so as you should do with the OSCP, I'll keep it simple.

I didnt change anything from my recon, I did take better notes from OSCP like machines from lainkusanagi's excel and even got some patterns about machines here and there, I finished Zephyr lab (that will be a topic for another post) and it helped me to not get rusty in AD but its definetely an overkill for this cert that could maybe play against you if you're not careful enough. Yes, you'll probably overthink stuff so mind your playground, we're in an entry certification.

## Exam

My exam started at 7pm, more than halfway through the AD set I got stuck around 11pm, decided to jump on a standalone and cracked it in around hour and a half, took a short break and came back to AD, something was missing so as I learnt from my previous exam attempts I kept it simple, re-did my enumeration and found the missing piece.

3am and I had AD down plus a standalone, went to sleep, got into the final machine around 4pm next day, the thing for this final machine is that I could have pwned it way faster if certain scan I left doing the night would have completed, but it didnt so I was missing information again, if you find a machine thats taking TOO long to finish scans or similars just reset it. Im ngl this pissed me a bit since it almost made me fail the exam and it wasnt even my fault, not a recon fault, not a thinking problem, just a structure issue.

## Final thoughts

After all, I had a passing score of 80/100, it was a great journey where I feel like I overprepared myself a bit and also posts from forums about the certification made me fear the exam a lot and my expectations in the difficulty were all time high. I'd 100% recommend the experience to any cybersec enthusiast just for the 24h exam since its a different experience if it wasnt for its price, OSCP became too pricey nowdays in my honest opinion.

Take away from this if you're giving it a try, keep it simple and remember, its an entry certification even if people say its insanely hard!! also please dont use their report template as it is, just copy and paste whatever you need from them and make a brand new document.
