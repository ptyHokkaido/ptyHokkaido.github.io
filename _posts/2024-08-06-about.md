## Whos behind this blog?

Im someone that started his CyberSecurity career eight years ago, while studying networking, operative systems and programming in school I always was learning cybersec by myself, it all started in small forums and Telegram groups, in 2017 HackTheBox came out and I started pwning machines there in 2018, thanks to Ippsec's channel I found the boxes really interesting since they looked like puzzles pretty much. 

Wanting to find vulnerabilities in the real world I hopped to do some Bug Bounty, I believe I've reports in HackerOne from 2018 or 2019 but didnt really pushed too much, got a bunch of dupes in programs like Sony, U.S. Dept Of Defense, Khan Academy... I did get small bounties in other BB programs that were not related to big BB platforms. Nowdays I rarely do BB but from time to time if I find an interesting program I do take my time with it since I find it fun and usually learn about a new technology stack.

After finishing my studies I worked as Help Desk, Web Application developer, Embedded software programmer for critical infraestructures and finally Pentester, Im proud of this path since I believe every position gave me knowledge to achieve my final goal which it was to get in this field as Red Team.

## What are the plans for the site?

Personally, its gonna help me to be more consistent at learning so I can post interesting stuff in here, topic is gonna be always Cybersec but I want to post a bit of everything, if I get to read a good cybersec book I'd like to make a post telling why I liked and recommend it for example, I pretend to upload some cheatsheets, some Bug Bounty finds, cool HTB boxes, talks about future goals and that kind of stuff.

I hope you guys can find something useful in my controlled chaos, cheers!
